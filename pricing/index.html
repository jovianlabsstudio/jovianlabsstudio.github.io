Questions

How do I pay?

Alerts: by card on Polar's secure checkout; we never see your card. Analysis tools: through your Apify account, which bills per use.

Is VAT included?

Prices are shown before VAT. Polar adds the VAT of your country at checkout; businesses can enter their VAT number there.

Which currency will I see?

Euros in Europe. Elsewhere, Polar may show the Pro plan in US dollars ($22 per month).

How do I cancel?

From the link at the bottom of every alert email (customer portal), in one click.

Is the image analysis an accredited measurement?

No. It is automated image analysis with its uncertainty stated, not an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited measurement.
