SEM scale bar & pixel-size check

Drop a folder of SEM or TEM images: the page reads the pixel size stored by Zeiss and Thermo Fisher (FEI) microscopes, finds and measures the scale bar in the data band, cross-checks the two, flags mixed magnifications and lets you download each image with a clean scale bar. Questions

Where is the pixel size stored in a Zeiss SEM TIFF?

In the private TIFF tag 34118 (CZ_SEM), a text block written by SmartSEM: the line AP_IMAGE_PIXEL_SIZE is followed by “Image Pixel Size = 2.233 nm”. If the image was saved at a different resolution than it was scanned, DP_IMAGE_STORE gives the stored size and the pixel size must be scaled.

And in a Thermo Fisher (FEI) TIFF?

In tag 34682 (or 34680 on older instruments), an INI-style text: the [Scan] section holds PixelWidth in metres (for example 1.953125e-009) and the [Image] section holds ResolutionX.

Why cross-check with the scale bar?

Images get resized, cropped or exported to PNG and lose their metadata; a mismatch between metadata and bar reveals a wrong file, a resized image or a calibration problem before it ruins a measurement.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser with JavaScript; the files are read locally and nothing is sent to a server. You can disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded.

Can it read the number printed next to the bar?

Not yet: type the bar length once for the whole folder (or leave it empty for original Zeiss or Thermo Fisher TIFF files, which carry their calibration).
