EU nanomaterial screening

Under Commission Recommendation 2022/C 229/01, a material meets criterion (a) when 50% or more of its particles, counted by number, have at least one external dimension between 1 and 100 nm. Each run reports the share of particles whose minimum Feret diameter is 100 nm or less, a Wilson confidence interval, a wider interval that adds calibration error and edge blur, the median minimum Feret, and a verdict: meets, not met, or borderline when the interval includes 50%. Questions

Which images can I use?

SEM or TEM images of one sample, all at the same magnification, as TIFF, PNG or JPG. Original Zeiss or Thermo Fisher TIFF files are calibrated from their metadata; otherwise give the scale bar length or the pixel size.

How many particles should I measure?

At least 300 for a size distribution and 500 for the nanomaterial screening. The report shows the precision reached on D50 and how many particles a ±2 % precision would need.

Is the nanomaterial verdict a regulatory determination?

No. It screens criterion (a) of the EU definition on the particles measured, with its uncertainty. It does not assess the volume specific surface area, dimensions below 1 nm or the thickness of platelets lying flat.
