About the studio

Jovian Labs is an independent studio that makes calm, honest puzzle games for the browser. Every level is checked by a solver before release, the games run on any phone or computer without installing anything, and the difficulty never changes to push players toward an ad.

Games

Monster Steps

Genre: Halloween puzzle · Released: 2026-10-25

A turn-based Halloween escape: every step you take, the monsters take theirs.

Lead a little witch to the glowing door, one tile at a time, while a mummy, a werewolf, a vampire and Anubis close in. After each of your steps, every monster takes its own: red claw marks show the tiles where you would be caught, and footprints show where each monster is about to go. Think ahead, wait at the right moment, and lure two monsters into each other to knock them out.

  • 33 levels over four haunted nights: graveyard, dark woods, castle and the tomb of Anubis
  • Four monsters with their own rules, introduced one at a time
  • Undo any step, even after being gobbled
  • Every level checked by a solver; par is the shortest way out

Counterpoise

Genre: Block puzzle · Released: 2026-10-24

A block puzzle on a balancing board: fill rows and columns, but don't tip it over.

Drag gem blocks onto an 8×8 grid and fill a row or a column to clear it. The board rests on a golden pivot: the farther a block sits from the centre, the more it weighs, and a move that would tip the board too far is refused. Hold a piece over the board and it leans before you let go.

  • 40 Journey levels around the moons of Jupiter, with a short playable tutorial
  • Classic endless mode and a Daily challenge with the same pieces for everyone
  • The board leans under the piece you hold, and weight marks show how heavy each column is
  • Fair draw: the pieces never depend on your board

Equipoise

Genre: Logic puzzle · Released: 2026-10-24

A relaxing logic puzzle: hang the weights until every mobile balances.

Drag numbered gem weights onto golden hooks until every bar hangs level. A bar balances when weight × distance is the same on both sides: the numbers above each bar do the maths for you and turn green when they match.

  • 40 levels in 4 chapters, from a single bar to deep nested mobiles with spare weights
  • A new Daily puzzle every day, the same for everyone
  • Undo, restart and hints whenever you are stuck
  • Calm ambient music and effects, each can be switched off
