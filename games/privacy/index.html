Privacy policy for our games

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who we are

Jovian Labs publishes the browser games Monster Steps, Counterpoise and Equipoise. For any question about this policy, write to contact@jovianlabsstudio.com.

No account, no personal details

Our games never ask for your name, email address or any account. You can play everything without signing in.

Your progress

The levels you have finished, your stars, best scores and settings (sound, music) are saved on your device, in your browser's storage, or in the save system of the platform where you play. They are not sent to us.

Gameplay statistics

On Playgama, the games send anonymous gameplay events through Playgama's analytics service: for example a level started or finished, the length of a session or the game's frame rate. They contain no name or contact details. We only see totals, which help us make levels fairer and fix problems.

Ads and gaming platforms

The games are free. On gaming platforms (Playgama, GamePix, GameMonetize, Y8, GameDistribution, CrazyGames), ads are shown by the platform and its advertising partners, who may use cookies or similar technologies and process data such as your IP address or device identifiers. This happens under each platform's own privacy policy and consent settings; we do not receive this data. The versions on itch.io and on our website show no ads.

Our website

Our website uses no advertising or analytics trackers. It remembers the language you choose in your browser. Like any website, the services that deliver it (GitHub Pages for hosting, Cloudflare for the domain name) may record technical data such as your IP address in their server logs.

Children

Our games are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Ad settings for younger players are managed by each platform.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about players, we usually cannot identify you. For data held by a gaming platform, please contact that platform. For anything else, write to contact@jovianlabsstudio.com and we will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
